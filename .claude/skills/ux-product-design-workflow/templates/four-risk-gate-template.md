## Risk Framework

Every product initiative faces four risks that must be addressed:

| Risk | Question | Owner |
|------|----------|-------|
| **Value** | Will users want this? | PM + Design |
| **Usability** | Can users figure it out? | Design |
| **Feasibility** | Can engineering build it? | Engineering |
| **Viability** | Does it work for the business? | PM |

## Gate Decision

### Decision Criteria

| Status | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| **Pass** | All four risks addressed (understood, with mitigation plan) |
| **Conditional Pass** | Risks understood, specific validation actions planned |
| **Incomplete** | One or more risks not yet assessed |
| **Fail** | Critical risk identified with no viable mitigation |
