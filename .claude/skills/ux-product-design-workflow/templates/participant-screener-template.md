## Screener Script

### Introduction

> "Hi, I'm [Name] from [Company]. We're conducting a research study about [topic] and we're looking for people who [criteria]. The session takes about [duration] and we offer [incentive] as a thank you for your time. Would you be interested in participating?"

### If Interested, Continue to Questions

> "Great! I have a few quick questions to make sure you're a good fit for this study. This will just take a couple of minutes."

### Questions Flow

**Q1:** "[Question text]"
- If [target answer]: "Great, thanks!" → Continue
- If [exclude answer]: "Thank you for your time. We have all the participants we need for your profile right now, but we'll keep you in mind for future studies."

**Q2:** "[Question text]"
- [Continue pattern...]

### Closing (Qualified)

> "You seem like a great fit for this study! Let me schedule your session. We have times available on [dates]. Which works best for you?"

### Closing (Not Qualified)

> "Thank you for your interest! We have all the participants we need for your profile at the moment, but we appreciate your time and will keep you in mind for future studies."
