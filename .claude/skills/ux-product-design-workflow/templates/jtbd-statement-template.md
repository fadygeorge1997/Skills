## JTBD Statement

### Canonical Format

> **When** [situation/context],
> **I want to** [motivation/job],
> **So I can** [expected outcome],
> **Without** [pain point/friction to avoid].

### Example

> **When** I receive my monthly salary,
> **I want to** pay all my bills in one session,
> **So I can** feel financially organized,
> **Without** worrying I've missed a payment or been overcharged.
